Frequently asked questions

Is what I say actually kept private?
Yes. Whatever you tell us stays between us — no names shared, nothing repeated to anyone in your life. This matters most for the things you can't say out loud to people who know you, and we take it seriously.
Is this therapy?
No. Confidein is a listening service, not a clinical or licensed therapy service. If you need professional mental health support, please seek a licensed provider.
Who will I talk to?
A real person — not a bot or an AI. We don't do in-app matching with strangers; every call is scheduled directly with us.
Will I be judged or told what to do?
No. If you choose "just listen," we won't offer advice unless you ask. If you're open to advice, we'll share our honest take when you want it.
How do I book a call?
Go to the Talk page, tell us your name and what you need, then reach out by email or GPay to schedule a time.
What if it's an emergency?
Please don't wait for a scheduled call. Contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country right away.
