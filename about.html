About Confidein

Confidein is a simple idea: sometimes you don't need therapy, an app, or a diagnosis — you need a real person to listen without judging you.

There's no matching algorithm and no chatbot. When you reach out, you're scheduling a real call with a real person, on your terms.

How it's different

You choose the shape of the conversation. Just want to talk and be heard? Say so. Open to hearing an honest opinion? Say that instead. Either way, we follow your lead.

No account, no app to install. You reach out by email or GPay, and we schedule a time that works.

What we're not

Confidein is not therapy, counseling, or emergency support. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line instead of waiting for a scheduled call.
